# Changelog

## v1.0 (2026-10-31)

- First release.
- One-page site driven by a single `config.js`: name, type, tagline, colors, optional hero photo.
- Sticky header with a tap-to-call button.
- Hero with "Book now" (any booking link) or "Call to book", plus "Get directions".
- Services with prices, customer reviews, opening hours, and an embedded Google map.
- Contact form that works through Formspree (free tier) or falls back to opening the visitor's email app.
- LocalBusiness structured data added automatically once name and address are set.
- Setup bar that lists any placeholders you haven't filled in yet.
- No build step, no frameworks, no tracking.
