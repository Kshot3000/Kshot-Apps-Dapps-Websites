Pickleball Score Keeper - Privacy Policy

Who we are

Pickleball Score Keeper is published by Kshot Applications (kshot.tech). This policy explains what the app does with information.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your match history, the match in progress and your settings only on your device. We do not run servers for this app, have no user accounts, and never receive this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google: your device advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change that choice any time under Settings > Privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and deletion

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete; local data is removed when you uninstall the app.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes (last updated 2026-10-31). Questions: kcaidevelopment@gmail.com or kshot.tech
