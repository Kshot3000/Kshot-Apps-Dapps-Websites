GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we build.

I'm not sure which package I need.

Start with your goal. A single offer may need one page; a business with several services may need a larger site. Use the brief below and choose “Help me decide.”

Can you improve something I already have?

Yes. Include the existing site or repo in your brief and explain what feels broken or dated. We will scope the most useful improvements first.

Will I own the code?

Source-code handoff and usage rights should be included in your written project scope. Third-party services and assets retain their own terms.

Does a dapp package include a token or contract audit?

No. Wallet interfaces and integrations are scoped separately from token creation, custom contracts, and independent security audits. We'll make that boundary explicit in the proposal.
