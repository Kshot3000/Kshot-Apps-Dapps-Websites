GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we start.

Is this the same as the AI work desk?

No. The services page sells fixed-price finished jobs (copy, transcripts, research, cleanup). This page is hands-on developer work — code, docs, tests, and review — scoped and quoted per project.

Do you work in our repo or yours?

Either. Upstream-style work follows your contribution rules: fork, branch, tests, signed commits, and a pull request your maintainers can review like any other.

What does a security review cover?

The scope is agreed in writing first. Reviews report findings tied to evidence — the case or test that proves each one — and anything we could not test is labelled untested. Aegis audits cover AI-agent setups specifically: the published suite run against your setup, extended with your attack surface. No review is a guarantee.

How do we start?

DM @kshot9000 on X with your repo and what you need. You will get a written scope and price before any work begins.
